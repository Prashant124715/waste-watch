⚠️ Compliance vs. Coverage Confidence Rule

Key Governance Principle: High compliance on a low coverage rate does not represent high-confidence hygiene performance.

Example Scenario: If an Area has 94% Compliance but only 12% Coverage (e.g. 6 of 50 rooms inspected), the sample size is inadequate. Its Confidence Rating is LOW, and its overall Health Score reflects the missing audit data.
  • High Confidence: ≥ 75% rooms inspected
  • Medium Confidence: 40% – 74% rooms inspected
  • Low Confidence: < 40% rooms inspected
